# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient with other people
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- owning our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them

Examples of behaviour that isn't acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when someone represents the project in public. Report unacceptable
behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/scottpaulbrown). Reports will be reviewed promptly
and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject
contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour
breaks this code.
